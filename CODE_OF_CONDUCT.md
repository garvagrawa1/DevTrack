# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give feedback that focuses on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainer.
